# What's New — 1.4.0

App Store "What's New in This Version" copy. This is only the **second public
release after 1.0** — 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 were never published — so this text
covers everything added since launch, not just 1.4's work.

Verified against `release/1.4.0`: every feature listed below is present in the
shipped build.

---

## Primary version (≈2,600 characters, within the 4,000 limit)

```
A big update. Vittora now works across your wrist, your Home Screen, and every
Apple device you own — and it still keeps your data on your devices, with no
accounts and no tracking.

APPLE WATCH
• A full Watch app: log an expense in seconds with the Digital Crown
• Complications and Smart Stack widgets for today's spending and budget left
• Your recent transactions, right on your wrist

WIDGETS AND SHORTCUTS
• Home Screen widgets for today's spending and budget remaining
• Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, with amounts hidden while your phone is locked
• Add an expense straight from a widget, without opening the app
• Siri support: ask what you've spent, or add an expense by voice
• Find any transaction through Spotlight search

CONTINUE ANYWHERE
• Handoff: start a transaction on iPhone and finish it on iPad or Mac

NEW REPORTS AND INSIGHTS
• 50/30/20 breakdown of needs, wants and savings
• Emergency fund tracker showing how many months you're covered for
• Subscription audit that surfaces what your recurring charges really cost
• Cash-flow forecast for the months ahead
• Export Monthly Overview and Annual Summary as PDF

MADE YOURS
• Hindi language support
• OLED black theme and a choice of accent colours
• Choose when reminders arrive, with quiet hours

INDIA
• Compliance tips for cash limits, GST registration, TDS on rent and large
  deposit reporting — based only on what you've recorded, on your device

ACCESSIBILITY
• Extensive VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and contrast work across the app
• Full keyboard navigation on iPad and Mac

SUPPORT
• Contact support from inside the app, with a diagnostic summary you can read
  in full before anything is sent — and it never includes your amounts, notes
  or payees

FIXES
• Accounts with transactions now explain clearly that they must be archived
  rather than deleted, and the message is no longer hidden behind the tab bar
• Reinstalling the app no longer creates duplicate categories
• Pickers now show the item you selected instead of the word "Selected"
• Face ID unlock no longer briefly draws over the authentication screen
```

both targets), so claiming them here would be wrong. Handoff, Spotlight,
Shortcuts, keyboard navigation and every report **are** verified on macOS.

## Notes for whoever publishes this

- The privacy line is deliberate: it is the app's main differentiator and the
  one claim reviewers and users both check.
- India-specific items are worth keeping in the en-IN listing; they can be cut
  from en-US if a shorter listing is wanted, but they do no harm.
- Do **not** mention the Watch version fix or the icon fix — those were build
  problems that never reached a user.
