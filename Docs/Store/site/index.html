Vittora

Your personal finance companion — private by design.

No bank linking. No tracking. No analytics of any kind — not even our own. Your data lives on your devices and in your personal iCloud.

What Vittora does

  • Track — fast transaction entry, receipt scanning (OCR), smart categorization, saved filters, full edit history.
  • Budget — monthly budgets with alerts at 75/90/100%, recurring transactions, cash-flow projection.
  • Split — group expenses with clean summaries and PDF sharing.
  • Save — savings goals plus contribution-headroom tracking.
  • Tax — built-in estimates as you go (U.S. federal estimate; India old-vs-new regime with 80C/80D/HRA planning). Estimates for planning only — not tax, legal, or investment advice.
  • Import — moving from another app? Import your CSV export in minutes.
  • Protect — Face ID app lock, encryption, offline-first storage with optional iCloud sync.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later · Mac with macOS 26 or later · Optional sync uses your Apple ID and iCloud.
