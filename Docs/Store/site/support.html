Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and — if you enable sync — in your personal iCloud. Vittora has no servers and no account system; we never see your data. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up my data?

Enable iCloud sync (Settings → iCloud Sync) so your data lives in your personal iCloud, and/or use Export to produce CSV/PDF copies of your records.

How do I move data from another app?

Export a CSV from your previous app, then use Transactions → ⋯ → Import CSV. Mint and YNAB export formats are recognized automatically; generic bank CSVs work too.

Why don't my devices show the same data?

Check that both devices are signed into the same Apple ID, iCloud sync is enabled in Vittora on both, and iCloud Drive is on in system settings. Sync can take a moment after changes.

Are the tax numbers exact?

No — tax figures are estimates for planning only (U.S.: federal estimate, state taxes not included; India: regime comparison with FY-aware slabs). Verify with a qualified professional before filing.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings → Delete All Data permanently erases your financial data and resets the app. If iCloud sync was on, also remove Vittora data from iCloud in system settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.

I found a bug

Email us with the steps to reproduce and a screenshot if possible. Since Vittora has no analytics or crash reporting, your reports are the only way we learn about problems — thank you!
