English listing for the same India storefront.

translated from it.

(DEC-013/014/015); Vittora Pro may now be described. The "no ads, no
trackers, no accounts, no data selling" claims remain true and must be kept.
INR examples and regime language per DEC-010 D5.

(`iphone-69-hi`, `iphone-65-hi`, `ipad-13-hi`, `watch-hi`) were already built.

## Notes for whoever publishes this

- **The app name keeps the Latin "Vittora".** It is the brand and it matches
  the icon, the Watch app title and every other storefront. Only the descriptor
  is transliterated.
- **Keywords mix scripts on purpose.** Hindi speakers searching the India
  storefront type both — *बजट* and "budget", *टैक्स* and "80c". Latin
  finance terms are left in Latin because that is how they are typed.
- **This is the India storefront**, so the tax and compliance content carries
  the same weight as it does in `metadata-en-IN.md` — it is the strongest
  India-specific reason to download.
- **Screenshots** are the 1.8.0 gallery in `Marketing/AppStore/` (beside the repo): iphone-69-hi, iphone-65-hi, ipad-13-hi, watch-hi. Regenerate with the scripts in `Scripts/store/` — see `Docs/Store/screenshots/README.md`.
