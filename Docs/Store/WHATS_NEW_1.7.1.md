# What's New — 1.7.1

App Store "What's New in This Version" copy. Users are coming from 1.7.0.
Six blocks: three languages x iOS/iPadOS and Mac. Paste each into the matching
localization in App Store Connect.

1.7.1 is a **paywall and entitlement clarity release**. The headline is the
rebuilt Pro paywall: plans are visible, Lifetime sits as a third plan, and the
trial label is correct. Family Sharing is stated on Annual and Lifetime. The
store stays out of the way when Apple payments are off. The Tax Estimator no
longer sends a free user through the profile form before showing the lock.

**The Mac copy is not the same as iOS this time.** It carries one extra line:
the sidebar selection now follows the chosen accent colour. That fix is macOS
only and has no iOS counterpart.

Deliberately not listed: CI, iOS 27 SDK floor work, StoreKit scheme paths,
Xcode warnings, test coverage, M2.4, saved filters, and investment planning.
None of that belongs in user-facing release notes.

No prices — they vary by storefront.

## iOS / iPadOS — English (en-US, en-IN) (722 characters)

```
PRO PAYWALL, REBUILT
• The Pro upgrade screen is clearer: Monthly, Annual, and Lifetime as a third plan
• The trial label shows correctly on Annual when you're eligible
• Family Sharing is stated on Annual and Lifetime

TAX ESTIMATOR
• Without Pro, the Tax Estimator now shows the upgrade option straight away instead of asking you to set up a profile first

WHEN APPLE PAYMENTS ARE OFF
• If Apple payments are turned off on the device — Screen Time or a managed device — the store stays hidden so you aren't sent into a purchase you can't complete

CLEARER ENTITLEMENTS
• Copy for family members and Lifetime access matches what you actually have

Still no accounts, no ads, no tracking — your data stays on your devices.
```

## Notes

- Limit is 4000 characters per locale; every block is well under.
- No prices. The trial is named only as a label-correctness point, never as an offer.
- "Lifetime as a third plan" is intentional: users upgrading from 1.7.0 should see
  Lifetime as a peer option beside Monthly and Annual, not as a separate button.
- Store-hidden-when-payments-off covers Screen Time and managed devices without
  naming internal entitlement APIs.
- The Mac-only APPEARANCE line claims the sidebar follows the accent colour.
  **Confirm that on the uploaded Mac build before submitting** — the fix shipped
  in #245 but was never seen running; the Mac app would not open a window on the
  build machine. If it did not take, cut that one bullet and the rest stands.
- Do not paste CI, iOS 27, StoreKit scheme paths, M2.4, saved filters, or
  investment planning into App Store Connect.
