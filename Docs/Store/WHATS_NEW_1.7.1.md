# What's New — 1.7.1

App Store "What's New in This Version" copy. Users are coming from 1.7.0.

1.7.1 is a **paywall and entitlement clarity release**. The headline is the
rebuilt Pro paywall: plans are visible, Lifetime sits as a third plan, and the
trial label is correct. Family Sharing is stated on Annual and Lifetime. The
store stays out of the way when Apple payments are off.

Deliberately not listed: CI, iOS 27 SDK floor work, StoreKit scheme paths, M2.4,
saved filters, investment planning, and other internal or unshipped items. None
of that belongs in user-facing release notes.

Content is the same on iOS/iPadOS and Mac. No prices — they vary by storefront.

PRO PAYWALL, REBUILT
• The Pro upgrade screen is clearer: Monthly, Annual, and Lifetime as a third plan
• The trial label shows correctly on Annual when you're eligible
• Family Sharing is stated on Annual and Lifetime

WHEN APPLE PAYMENTS ARE OFF
• If Apple payments are turned off on the device — Screen Time or a managed device — the store stays hidden so you aren't sent into a purchase you can't complete

CLEARER ENTITLEMENTS
• Copy for family members and Lifetime access matches what you actually have

Still no accounts, no ads, no tracking — your data stays on your devices.

## Notes

- Limit is 4000 characters per locale; all three are well under.
- No prices. Trial is named only as a label-correctness point, not a price.
- "Lifetime as a third plan" is intentional: users upgrading from 1.7.0 should see
  Lifetime as a peer option beside Monthly and Annual.
- Store-hidden-when-payments-off covers Screen Time and managed devices without
  naming internal entitlement APIs.
- Do not paste CI, iOS 27, StoreKit scheme paths, M2.4, saved filters, or
  investment planning into App Store Connect.
