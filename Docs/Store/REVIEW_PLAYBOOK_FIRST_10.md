# First-10 honest App Store reviews playbook

Draft only — do not publish ask-posts until Rahul approves. No farms. No “review for a gift.” No paid review schemes. Measurement: App Store Connect ratings only (US + IN). Current baseline before 1.7.1: 0 ratings.

Goal: ~10 honest ratings after 1.7.1 is live, enough to unlock Product Hunt timing later — not to manufacture stars.

---

## Principles

1. Only people who actually used Vittora.
2. Never trade Pro, codes, cash, or merch for a review.
3. Never script star ratings; you may remind that a review helps, not what to say.
4. Privacy-first: do not ask anyone to paste screenshots of their ledger or amounts.
5. Separate “rate the app” from “write a review” — either helps ASC.

---

## Day 1.7.1 is live (same day / first 48 hours)

**Do**

- Soft in-person / DM asks to people who already installed and used 1.7.0 or updated to 1.7.1: friends, TestFlight alumni who opted in to product updates, colleagues who aren’t on the payroll for reviews.
- Point them at the live App Store listing (US and/or IN storefront matching their Apple ID).
- One sentence ask, e.g.: “If Vittora’s been useful, an App Store rating helps other Apple users find a private option — only if you mean it.”
- Offer a support path (`support@vittora.app`) if something broke on update — fix first, ask later.

**Don’t**

- Post “leave a 5-star review” on X / IG / FB / YouTube as the first public CTA.
- Bundle review asks with offer codes or Lifetime discounts.
- Ask employees / contractors as a deliverable.
- Use review farms, Telegram “ASO services,” or reciprocal-review groups.

**Target for day 0–2:** a handful of genuine ratings from known users (even 2–4), not 10 overnight.

---

## What needs an in-app ask later (after 1.7.1 settles)

Hold until product ships a proper prompt (Priya / Rahul / Claude Code), claim-safe:

- Trigger only after clear value (e.g. N days of use, a completed budget, Year in Review opened, successful Pro restore — exact rules TBD).
- Never on first launch, never on paywall dismiss, never after a failed purchase.
- Use Apple’s `SKStoreReviewController` / requestReview — system may no-op; that’s fine.
- Copy: invite feedback, do not beg for five stars; link to support for problems.

Until that ships: manual/honest outreach only.
