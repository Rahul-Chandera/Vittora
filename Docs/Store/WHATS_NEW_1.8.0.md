# What's New — 1.8.0

App Store "What's New in This Version" copy. Users are coming from 1.7.1.

1.8.0 is the largest **feature release** since launch, so the copy is grouped by
what a user can do rather than listed by feature number. Tax leads: it went from
two countries to five, and every country's rules were corrected in the
2026-10-03 tax logic review.

**iOS/iPadOS vs Mac.** The section "ON IPHONE AND APPLE WATCH" (Live Activities,
the interactive widget, Watch voice entry) describes iPhone-only features.
**Delete that section from the Mac listing**; everything else applies to both.

AI is named only for the two features that are AI — on-device category
suggestions and the Apple Intelligence month summary ("on devices that support
it") — never for the rules-based health score, outlook or insight cards.

Vittora Pro is named only where a feature is Pro (`ReportType.requiresPro`, the
tax dashboard's `proGated`), so a free user is not promised something locked.

Deliberately not listed:
- **Apple Wallet import (M3.7.1).** It ships switched off until Apple grants the
  FinanceKit entitlement.
- The CI, test and accessibility-audit work, and the bugs that were introduced
  and fixed within this same release cycle (#297, the iPad tint, the Mac sidebar).
- The CloudKit Production schema fix as such. Its user-facing effect is listed
  in plain words under Fixes.

## English (2366 characters)

```
TAX FOR FIVE COUNTRIES
• Tax estimates now cover the United Kingdom (with Scottish rates and National Insurance), Australia (with the Medicare levy and low income tax offset) and Canada (federal plus all 13 provinces and territories, with CPP and EI), alongside India and the US
• Every country's rules were reviewed and corrected, and the 2026 tax years added, including India's marginal relief and employer NPS, and US capital-loss netting, self-employment tax and the new senior deduction
• See what an extra contribution would save, compare 80C options and track investment maturity dates
• Estimates and the regime comparison are part of Vittora Pro; your tax profile stays free

NEW REPORTS AND INSIGHTS
• Net worth over time
• Financial health score, and a spending outlook with what-if scenarios (Vittora Pro)
• Reports flag unusual spending in a category and suggest budgets to trim or raise
• Category suggestions learn from your own history, on your device
• Monthly Overview opens with a plain-language summary of your month, written by Apple Intelligence on devices that support it

HOUSEHOLD SHARING
• Share budgets with the people you live with through iCloud: shared budgets, spending by member, and control over who can edit

ON IPHONE AND APPLE WATCH
• Live Activities and the Dynamic Island: a running total in Shopping Mode, counted against a budget if you like, and a countdown to your next bill
• An interactive widget logs a preset expense without opening the app
• On Apple Watch, say "500 for groceries" to add an expense

EVERYDAY MONEY
• Sinking funds: several savings goals can share one account, and Vittora shows when they claim more than it holds
• Sub-categories, one level deep, grouped under their parent in lists and reports
• Scan a multi-page document into a single attachment

FIXES
• Net worth now subtracts credit card balances instead of adding them
• Amounts ending in zero keep their cents
• Recurring income rules create income, and your salary no longer counts as spending in Monthly Spend or the cash flow projection
• Category Breakdown now includes uncategorised spending
• iCloud sync now covers documents, split groups and some account, debt and savings details it was missing
• Smoother iPad and Mac layouts, text fields and toolbar buttons

Still no accounts, no ads, no tracking — your data stays on your devices.
```

## Spanish — es-MX (2836 characters)

HOGAR COMPARTIDO

• El desglose por categoría ahora incluye los gastos sin categoría
• La sincronización con iCloud ahora incluye documentos, grupos de gastos compartidos y algunos datos de cuentas, deudas y ahorros que faltaban
