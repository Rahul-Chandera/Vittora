# What's New — 1.7.0

App Store "What's New in This Version" copy. Users are coming from 1.6.0.

1.7.0 is a **feature release**: Vittora Pro is the headline, so the copy leads
with it and is explicit about what stays free. That framing is deliberate —
the previous listing promised "every feature, every device", and a user
upgrading from 1.6.0 needs to see immediately that nothing they already made
was taken away (DEC-015).

The rest is ordered by what a 1.6.0 user actually notices: budgets that used to
expire after one period, and the transaction-type filter that could show income
inside a budget's spending.

Content is the same on iOS/iPadOS and Mac — unlike 1.6.0, this release has no
platform-specific user-facing work. The macOS paywall sizing and loading fixes
are not called out; nobody saw the Mac paywall before this release.

Deliberately not listed: the paywall's own colour and layout corrections, the
accessibility-audit exemptions, and the CI work. None of it means anything to a
user, and several are fixes to things introduced within this same release.

## English (1514 characters)

```
VITTORA PRO
• An optional upgrade that unlocks the forward-looking analysis: full tax planning and regime comparison, custom reports with PDF export, cash-flow forecast, subscription audit, the 50/30/20 report, the emergency fund tracker, debt analytics and unlimited receipt scanning
• Your records, your splits, iCloud sync and CSV export stay free, always — and everything you have already created stays yours
• Monthly, yearly with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time Lifetime purchase
• Without Pro you keep five receipt scans a month, and there are no limits on accounts or budgets

BUDGETS THAT KEEP GOING
• Monthly, weekly and yearly budgets now roll forward into the next period instead of quietly expiring after one
• Two active budgets can no longer be created for the same category, which was double-counting your spending

FIXES
• Filtering transactions by a single type no longer returned the wrong set, which could show income inside a budget's spending
• The Notes field no longer dismisses the keyboard after every character
• Settings now shows your real iCloud sync status instead of a setting that did nothing
• The Dashboard refreshes when you change something in another tab
• Counts read "1 account" and "2 accounts" rather than "1 account(s)"

CLEARER DELETING
• Delete buttons are red again everywhere, and every confirmation offers Cancel
• The emergency fund report no longer fades its text into the navigation bar

Still no accounts, no ads, no tracking — your data stays on your devices.
```

## Notes

- Limit is 4000 characters per locale; all three are well under.
- Terminology is taken from the shipped string catalogue, not translated fresh,
  so the release notes and the app agree: *presupuestos*, *gastos compartidos*,
  *análisis de deudas*, *Panel*; *बजट*, *स्प्लिट*, *उधार विश्लेषण*, *डैशबोर्ड*.
- No prices. They vary by storefront and App Store Connect has its own fields.
  The 7-day trial is named because it is a material term, and it is annual-only
  (DEC-013).
- "no limits on accounts or budgets" is worth stating: the caps that existed in
  the 1.7.0 plan were removed (DEC-014), so a free user is not worse off than
  they were in 1.6.0.
