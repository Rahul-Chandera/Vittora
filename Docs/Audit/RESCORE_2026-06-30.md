## 5. Bottom line

Vittora has gone from **un-shippable** (2026-06-27) to **beta-ready** (2026-06-28) to — now — **a fully-remediated codebase with the entire audit backlog merged and green.** Correctness, security, architecture, and multi-platform support are genuinely strong (8–9), and the financial-integrity core that anchored the original NO GO is solid and well-tested.

The remaining distance to a **public** launch is no longer about code. It's four non-engineering calls: **(a)** the deployment-target/market-reach decision, **(b)** real-device Secure-Enclave + macOS-host verification, **(c)** App Store metadata + the Low accessibility polish, and **(d)** the GTM strategy (Epic M) including the viral-loop completion.

**Recommendation: ship the closed TestFlight beta now; gate the public launch on the §4 non-engineering conditions — principally the OS-floor/market decision and on-device verification.** The engineering remediation is, for practical purposes, **done.**
