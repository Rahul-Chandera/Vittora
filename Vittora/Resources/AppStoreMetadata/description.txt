Vittora is a private personal finance app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Track spending, income, budgets, recurring payments, savings goals, debt, tax estimates, receipts, and reports. Your data stays on your devices with optional iCloud sync through your Apple account — no analytics SDKs or ad trackers.

Shipped in this release:
- Accounts, transactions, transfers, and categories
- Budgets with spending alerts and recurring transaction rules
- Debt tracking, bill reminders, and local notifications you control
- Receipt attachments, document storage, and reports
- Tax estimates for India and the United States
- App Lock with Face ID / Touch ID and encrypted document storage
- iCloud sync across your Apple devices

Vittora is designed to feel calm, fast, and trustworthy so you can focus on your money decisions instead of wrestling with your tools.
